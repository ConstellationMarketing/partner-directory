Story First is a boutique video production studio built specifically for law firms. Founded by Chris, a former producer at a major legal video marketing company, Story First takes a strategy-first approach — every video is engineered to drive outcomes, not just look good.

## About This Partnership

Constellation and Story First are natural partners. We drive traffic and generate leads through SEO and paid ads; Story First gives those visitors a reason to call. Their video libraries pair directly with the landing pages, practice area pages, and Google Ads campaigns we manage for law firm clients.

## What Makes Story First Different

Most legal video companies focus on aesthetics. Story First focuses on what the video is supposed to do — and builds the shoot, the script (or guided conversation), and the delivery strategy around that goal. Clients receive complete video libraries plus deployment guides that map content to specific marketing channels.

Their clients span PI, family law, and criminal defense firms across the country. Consistent feedback: they make attorneys comfortable on camera and extract authentic stories that actually resonate with prospective clients.
