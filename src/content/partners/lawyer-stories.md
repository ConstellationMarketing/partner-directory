Lawyer Stories is one of the legal industry's most active communities — a podcast, platform, and networking hub built for attorneys who want to grow personally and professionally. With 250+ podcast episodes, free CLE credits, and a global member network, Lawyer Stories reaches legal professionals at every stage of their career.

## About This Partnership

Constellation's partnership with Lawyer Stories connects us to a community of attorneys who are actively engaged in building their practices. Their preferred partners program surfaces our services to an audience already invested in professional growth — a natural fit for law firms ready to invest in serious marketing.

## What Lawyer Stories Offers

The platform spans podcast content featuring interviews with attorneys and law firm leaders, CLE programming, a member community (Lawyer Stories Connect), events, and a curated partner directory. For law firms, visibility inside the Lawyer Stories ecosystem means reaching attorneys who take their business development seriously.
