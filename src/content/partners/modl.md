The Missouri Organization of Defense Lawyers (MODL) has been the professional home for Missouri's civil defense bar since 1984. The organization provides continuing legal education, hosts the annual John L. Oliver, Jr. Trial Academy at MU School of Law, files amicus curiae briefs, and runs regional networking events across the state.

## About This Partnership

Constellation's partnership with MODL connects us directly with Missouri's civil defense attorney community. Defense firms face unique marketing challenges — their clients are corporate and insurance entities, not individuals — and Constellation's expertise in legal marketing helps them build the visibility and reputation that drives referrals and direct inquiries.

## MODL's Mission

MODL is committed to ensuring fair and impartial treatment for defendants across Missouri's courts and legislature. The organization upholds high standards of professionalism and courtroom civility among its members — the same standard Constellation holds in serving law firm clients.
