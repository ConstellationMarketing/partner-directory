The Missouri Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers (MACDL) is the leading professional organization for criminal defense attorneys in Missouri. As an accredited CLE provider, legislative advocate, and community hub, MACDL supports attorneys dedicated to protecting the constitutional rights of the criminally accused.

## About This Partnership

Criminal defense is one of the most competitive practice areas in digital marketing — every city has dozens of firms fighting for the same search terms. Constellation's partnership with MACDL gives Missouri criminal defense attorneys access to proven marketing strategies that have driven results for criminal defense firms nationally.

## MACDL's Work

MACDL provides accredited CLE programming, operates a political action committee for legislative advocacy, files amicus briefs on key appellate matters, and hosts an annual awards ceremony recognizing outstanding attorneys and judges. The organization's Spring CLE events and member resource library keep Missouri's criminal defense bar at the leading edge of practice — including emerging topics like AI integration in legal practice.
